Will Your Swimmer Be Attending?

Since starting our Barracuda program, it's been a goal of ours to hold a bigger meet where our swimmers get to compete against other British Swim School Families swimmers across North Texas — at a pool with proper dive blocks and backstroke flags. We're excited to hold this Joint-Franchise meet at the Denton Natatorium on Saturday, October 17 at 6:30 PM.

We want every family to respond, even if your swimmer won't be attending. Please complete this form no later than September 15 — swimmers not registered by then will not be able to participate in the meet.

Swimmer registration is $15 and will be charged to your family account 14 days before the meet, once the final team roster is set. The $5 per person spectator fee is paid on-site at the door. Families are responsible for their own transportation to and from the meet.
